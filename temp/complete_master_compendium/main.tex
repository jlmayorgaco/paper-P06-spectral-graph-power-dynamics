\begin{abstract}
This compendium is intentionally redundant.  It does not replace the IEEE
Transactions manuscript or the shorter unified-theory note.  It preserves both,
then appends the verification artifacts that led to the present claim
calibration.  The purpose is to avoid losing equations, figures, negative
results, diagrams, and gate reports while the research direction is being
refined.
\end{abstract}

\section*{How to Read This Compendium}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{How to Read This Compendium}

The document has four layers.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Part I} reproduces the unified theory note that integrates the
Gemini proposals under calibrated claims: damping-operator hierarchy,
controller-aware rational bridge, protected resolvent, flow-feasible
retrofitting, weak nodes, weak links, and open gates.
\item \textbf{Part II} reproduces the full IEEE manuscript that already contains
the detailed derivations, equations, ANDES sections, diagrams, claim-readiness
tables, appendices, and reviewer checklist.
\item \textbf{Part III} collects the main figures that should remain visible
even if the paper is later split into multiple submissions.
\item \textbf{Part IV} appends the raw gate reports, so every number in the
theory has a traceable validation status.
\end{enumerate}

The governing claim discipline is unchanged: a result is not called original or
validated merely because the algebra is correct.  The tables and reports below
separate verified claims, model-reduced claims, ANDES/NEP claims, negative
controls, and open gates.
