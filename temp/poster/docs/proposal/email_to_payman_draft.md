Poster title:
A Coupling-Diagnostic Spectral Screen for Damping-Margin Planning in Inverter-Dominated Industrial Power Grids

Student:
Jorge L. Mayorga Taborda
Universidad de los Andes, Bogota, Colombia

Research summary:
The work develops a damping-margin planning diagnostic for inverter-dominated power grids. It shows that classical graph-frequency and line-reinforcement screens can recommend actions that raise modal stiffness while reducing the true full-QEP damping margin. The proposed contribution is a rational graph-filter correction based on off-diagonal modal damping coupling, with reduced/full QEP fallback when perturbation screening is unsafe. Preliminary results include a reproducible six-bus reversal case, an IEEE39-derived surrogate showing 35/46 line-reinforcement reversals, and reduced-model estimator tests.
