   We linearize the network around an operating point and split the full
ANDES state matrix into retained network and interface states on one
side, and condensed controller and auxiliary states on the other.
Eliminating the condensed block by a Schur complement leaves a
frequency-dependent self-energy, Σ(s) = D0 + C(sI − Acc)−1B, so the
retained system becomes a rational nonlinear eigenvalue problem T(s) =
s2M + sΣ(s) + L, with L the inertia-normalized network Laplacian.
Unlike a static reduction, Σ(s) keeps the controller dynamics that carry
the damping. Zeros are extracted by Beyn contour integration over the
