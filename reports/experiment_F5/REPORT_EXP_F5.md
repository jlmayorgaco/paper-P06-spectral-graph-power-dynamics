# Experiment F5 — Self-energy diagnostics

**F5_STATUS: DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY**

The exact frequency-dependent Schur self-energy Γ_k at the frozen C0
critical pole was computed in F2 for the heterogeneous analytic IEEE-39
port transfer. Its per-mode complex entries and zero Schur reconstruction
residual are retained in F2 TABLE_F03. The comparison confirms that modal
coupling must be represented through the full Schur complement.

No graph-controller correction was applied: F4 did not produce a coefficient
seed θ_0. Therefore the residual Jacobian correction, pathway change, and
performance recovery ratios are not available. This result retains the
self-energy/pathway route without making a graph spectral gain-law claim.
