## Gate discipline

1. Discovery results cannot pass a holdout gate.
2. A tolerance changed after holdout inspection invalidates the affected gate.
3. A model correction after EMT inspection invalidates and restarts the affected
   blind test; it is never folded silently into the original prediction.
4. A negative result remains in the ledger and constrains the manuscript claim.
