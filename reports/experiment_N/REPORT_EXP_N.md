**Status: PASS_LOCAL_CERTIFIED.** The frozen analytical map passed its independent mixed-device identity gates before optimization. A fixed-support active-set calculation then found a locally KKT-certified nominal candidate. PowerDynamics and six nonlinear pulse runs passed after the candidate was hashed. ## Milestone A: equilibrium → ports → full poles

The installed SG and SimpleGFLDC equations yield a closed-form GFL trim at the frozen voltage and full-dispatch per-unit current. `iset_q`, `P_dc`, PLL angle, CC integrators, filter current, and DC states are set by those equations; Kp and Ki remain design variables. The original SG states and references carry into a retained SG with its scaled rating. A three-state local Newton correction removes floating-point cancellation in the surviving SG at bus 31, ρ=0.99, without changing voltage, dispatch, or gains. Its raw residual fell from 1.93×10⁻¹⁰ to 1.16×10⁻¹¹. All 60 single-bus trim cases now pass the 10⁻¹⁰ residual gate and P/Q and physical-bound checks. Maximum observed trim residual is 1.75×10⁻¹¹; maximum P and Q errors are 1.04×10⁻¹² and 9.99×10⁻¹⁴ pu. See [TABLE_N04](TABLE_N04_trim_validation.csv).

The PD-free analytical model forms physical SG/GFL ports from the installed equations, adds the exact initialized ZIP/network closure, eliminates algebraic voltages, and quotients the rotational gauge. It does not call the old ExpK mixed model. Sixty-eight withheld cases include every generator bus at ρ = 0.10, 0.50, 0.90, 0.99, gain-bound variants, corrected ExpE/G/K architectures, and five deterministic random mixed ρ/K vectors. The separate development point at bus 38, ρ=0.50 is excluded from the withheld maxima. Against independently compiled PowerDynamics at the **same explicit trim**, the maximum withheld reduced-A relative error is 3.20×10⁻¹⁵, maximum port relative error is 1.78×10⁻¹², and maximum complete matched-pole error is 5.56×10⁻⁸ s⁻¹. ## Milestone B: support-aware nominal design

The primary objective minimizes retained initialized generator MW while every physical finite pole satisfies `Re λ ≤ −0.05 s⁻¹`. No PowerDynamics call occurs in the optimizer. All-GFL violates the margin; all-SG has α = −0.0980654 s⁻¹. Ten single-SG supports were scanned through architecture-aware continuation under four deterministic gain patterns, yielding 40 corrected feasible boundaries. The branch ledger records the complete spectral abscissa and active-mode changes. A 1,980-point budget audit covered all 45 two-SG supports. All 1,024 SG support architectures were additionally evaluated under two deterministic allocations at a cost below the incumbent; no cheaper sampled feasible point was found. These finite screens do **not** establish completeness of every continuous branch. ## Independent post-freeze validation

PowerDynamics was rebuilt at the **hashed, unchanged** Z*. The trimmed equilibrium residual is 5.40×10⁻¹²; P/Q errors are around 10⁻¹⁵ pu and declared internal checks pass. Its physical rightmost pole is −0.050000000138500054 s⁻¹. The analytical–PD alpha difference is 9.67×10⁻¹⁰ s⁻¹, the worst complete finite-pole matching error is 4.28×10⁻⁸ s⁻¹, the reduced-A relative error is 7.56×10⁻¹⁷, and the critical right eigenvector overlap is 1.0. **PD validation passes**, with only about 1.4×10⁻¹⁰ s⁻¹ of measured headroom beyond the required margin. [TABLE_N13](TABLE_N13_powerdynamics_postfreeze_validation.csv), [FIG_N03](FIG_N03_PD_vs_analytic_poles.png).

Six nonlinear PowerDynamics pulses used 10⁻⁵ and 2×10⁻⁵ of the frozen loads at buses 8, 16, and 29 (the latter near SG bus 38). All solves succeeded. Maximum frequency scaling error is 0.139%, maximum voltage scaling error is 0.0011%; **small-pulse TDS validation passes**. Bus 8 is worst among these tested locations, with about 0.012584 Hz/s per MW peak COI RoCoF and 0.003010 Hz per MW peak frequency deviation. The slow critical mode did not meet a 2% settling criterion within the 60 s simulation horizon. [TABLE_N14](TABLE_N14_time_domain_validation.csv), [trajectories](TABLE_N14_trajectories.csv), [FIG_N08](FIG_N08_nonlinear_frequency_RoCoF_TDS.png).

Using ExpG's frozen 0.5 Hz/s RoCoF and 0.5 Hz excursion limits **only as post-design diagnostics**, small-pulse linear extrapolation at bus 8 gives 39.73 MW and 166.10 MW capacities, respectively. Those powers were not tested nonlinearly; this is not a large-disturbance security claim. The previous ExpG 100 MW design event exceeds the estimated RoCoF capacity of this nominal replacement optimum. [Capacity table](TABLE_N14_disturbance_capacity.csv).

## Mechanism and limits

At the active −0.05 s⁻¹ mode, the exact port Schur decomposition chooses pivot bus 36. The derivative of the pole with respect to ρ38 is +106.434487 s⁻¹ per fraction; essentially all of it enters through the collective self-energy term at that pivot, with 1.43×10⁻⁶ absolute decomposition discrepancy (about 1.35×10⁻⁸ relative). Direct Kp/Ki sensitivity at pivot 36 and collective sensitivity from bus 38 are separately resolved. The physical graph's `Re(Y_port)` and `Im(Y_port)` do not commute (normalized commutator 0.0404). Bus 38 ranks 32nd of 39 by graph strength, so simple graph-strength ranking does not explain its retention value. The constant box-corner gain pattern has no meaningful gain-strength correlation. No `K=h(L)` law is claimed. [TABLE_N15](TABLE_N15_self_energy_explanation.csv), [pathways](TABLE_N15_pairwise_pathways.csv), [graph diagnostic](TABLE_N15_graph_diagnostic.csv), [FIG_N07](FIG_N07_self_energy_direct_vs_collective.png).

The principal limits are continuous branch incompleteness across the support lattice, tiny PD margin headroom, and the absence of nonlinear validation at extrapolated disturbance capacities. Structured-K and robust β optimization were optional secondary studies and were not run. The installed GFL device has no validated hard current limiter, so no current-limit safety claim is made. No push was performed.
