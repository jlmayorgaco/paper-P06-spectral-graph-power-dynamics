## D. Narrative / theory risks a judge will probe
1. **Delays and $\alpha_\perp$.** With PLL delay the system is a DDE; $\alpha_\perp$ must then be the rightmost root of $\det\Delta_\perp(s)$, not an eigenvalue of a matrix. Panel 2 must say "(delay-free chart; with delay: rightmost characteristic root)". The H4 witness has no PLL delay; keep it delay-free and say so.
2. **Full-model evidence vs. n-hop claim.** The full PowerDynamics IEEE-39 campaign (exact delay) found: first crossing 41.57 ms; sparse 4-site retuning (incl. {30,33,36,37}) fails; a uniform 14 % $K_p$ cut restores the margin and passes 5/5 events. The n-hop results are 9-bus + reduced IEEE-39 (exploratory). The title "Information-Radius Co-Design" is therefore supported only at 9-bus scale: panel 8 must carry the "exploratory / declared sweep / not certified" labels, and panel 9 should cite the full-model delay result as the validated robustness layer.
3. **"More information gives more control authority"** is true for the nested free n-hop class (ε non-increasing) only; it is false as a statement about graph-polynomial controllers. The footer line is acceptable only because panel 7 states the nesting condition.
4. **Footer headline** ("Preserving one mode does not protect the grid") is the panel-6 message; it is consistent with the full-model result (exact transport keeps one mode, others move).
5. **$D_H$ positivity** at one frequency is never a stability certificate — keep the sentence in panel 5.
6. **$M_{\rm eff}$** is inertial-like, not rotor inertia; even/odd split and graph filters are PROPOSED.
