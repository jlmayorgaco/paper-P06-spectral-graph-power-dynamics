## 3. Negative results preserved

- **F10 prose.** Additive 30/52 is superseded by 31/52 under the current
  truth. Modal sensitivity 22 and pairwise 38 are unchanged.
- **Witness robustness.** H = {H4} survives in only 34 % (E37 fleet-wide),
  69 % (per unit) and 62 % (combined) of envelope draws.
  - Each of M ±20 %, x′ ±10 %, KA ±15 % and TE ±15 % alone flips it, either
    to ∅ or to κ = 3.
- **R4 direction metric.** Fleet-wide it is 0.76, below the preregistered
  0.9. The failures are draws already stable at P4, where a different mode is
  rightmost.
- **Finite steps.** The linear port prediction mis-ranks finite policy steps
  across coordinates (Spearman 0.2). The one-step g estimate is 3× short.
- **Lines.** No single line reinforcement up to 2× restores P4. The
  finite-step sign agreement of the line derivative is 9/12.
- **Census.** 124 of 139 unstable census portfolios have a frozen α⊥ above
  1 s⁻¹ (up to 1276 s⁻¹), far from the electromechanical margins of the core
  lattices, so the census MAE/RMSE are not meaningful and only sign and ranking
  metrics are. Additive and pairwise α-truncations are
  anti-predictive on census size 4 (ROC-AUC 0.14–0.15). No lower-order method
  recovers the census minimal sets.
- **Closure diagnostics.** Both closure distance and \|χ\| mis-rank the clean
  counterfactual: the stable G_S looks riskier than the unstable P4.
- **Cumulant hypotheses.** The connected-cumulant campaign's hypotheses
  (old claims C10 and C11: witness contraction as a change of connected
  support, and |χ| suppression as the design target) remain refuted (V24).
  The flagship remains composite (V09).

## 5. Scientific decision (Phase 13)

The final report in the session gives the answers to Q1–Q15, followed by Q16
once Q1–Q15 are closed. They are persisted here verbatim in short form.

1. **F10 after the re-audit.**
   - No F10 truth label changes.
   - Exact H on the 52 points: modal sensitivity 22, additive 31, pairwise
     38, third-order 50.
   - On the 36-point line: 3, 0, 22, 36.
   - Subset-level sign accuracy: 0.75, 0.87, 0.97, 0.996.
2. **Screens that approve H4 at P4.** Single, pair and triple screening;
   first-order modal sensitivity (−0.203); additive (−0.213); pairwise
   (−0.210); third-order α-truncation (−0.184); and the order-≤2
   determinant truncation (−0.015). Only the order-≤3 determinant truncation
   and the exact closure flag it.
3. **Strongest fair baseline.**
   - For a portfolio that is explicitly evaluated: full eigenanalysis (the
     oracle).
   - Among non-enumerating predictors: the third-order truncation (50/52,
     pooled AUC 0.995). It costs 15 of 16 eigen-analyses and still misses P4.
   - Among model-free screens: replaced MVA (census AUC 0.81).
   - The oracle-assisted closure distance (0.94) needs ω\* and is not a fair
     competitor.
4. **Beyond exhaustive eigenanalysis.** The framework adds no accuracy. It
   adds:
   - an exact reduced representation (one common realization; identity
     3e-10);
   - H and κ with any-order planning semantics;
   - policy maps;
   - principal-minor attribution;
   - boundary derivatives with Newton to the exact boundary.
5. **Clean g-only counterfactual.** Yes: only g changes, and k, t and h are
   fixed.
6. **What stays and what changes.**
   - Identical: Ybus, locations, ratings, scheduled P/Q, voltages (2.5e-8),
     k, t, h, and all static metrics.
   - Changed: α, critical frequency, RHP count, H, κ, and every dynamic
     predictor.
7. **Non-composability under uncertainty.** Mostly: 0.88, 1.00, 0.84 and
   0.62 (fleet-wide PARTIAL).
8. **The exact witness.** No under machine uncertainty (0.34–0.69); yes under
   converter stress (1.00).
9. **κ = 4.** It is the nominal-data value. Under machine perturbations it is
   often 3 or absent.
10. **Custom GFL reproduction.** Yes, with the same equations in ANDES; GATE
    5 PASS.
11. **What stays conditional.** Model adequacy (custom GFL, phasor, no current
    limits), the witness (machine data), the GFL line ranking (converter
    model) and governor dependence. I01 depends on accepting the pandapower
    fix.
12. **Design value.** Yes: direction, ranking and exact boundaries for g and
    k. Not finite magnitudes, and no single line fixes P4.
13. **Strongest scientific claim.** Replacement-portfolio compatibility is
    non-composable. The exact minimal incompatibility sets H(θ) can be a
    complete portfolio whose every proper subset is safe. They are
    reorganized by converter policy alone while every static network quantity
    is unchanged. Exact network closure of local device changes produces
    this and gives exact boundary derivatives.
14. **Strongest practical claim.**
    - Lower-order subset screening and static/aggregate screens can approve an
      unsafe portfolio, so check the complete portfolio at the intended
      converter policy.
    - The port derivative plus Newton identifies and locates the restoring
      control boundary.
15. **Blocking issues for TPWRS.** None scientifically. The three author
    decisions (memo) and the wording discipline in V03, V16 and V21 remain.

## 7. Methodological observation (outside the claims)

> Dominant observed modal energy need not correspond to the stability-limiting
> eigenmode.

**Source and scope.** This comes from the ParaEMT SQ1 modal-library and
estimator-qualification work, using linear residues of the frozen phasor models
and synthetic signals built from them.
- It is **not** an IEEE-39 EMT result.
- It is not a TX4 claim.

**Evidence.** In 10 of 26 frozen models:
- the rightmost (stability-limiting) in-band transverse mode, the 0.91-Hz
  family, carries a minority of the channel energy under the preregistered
  pulse;
- a 0.64-Hz family, which decays 0.003–0.07 s⁻¹ faster, carries 85–92 % of the
  median channel energy.

**Effect on the estimator.** The frozen single-mode estimator resolved 40.7 % of
such templates, against 96.5 % of the others (post hoc).
