# CLAIMS

Status vocabulary, in decreasing strength: **EXACT IDENTITY** (holds to roundoff,
failure is a bug) · **THEOREM** (proved, not merely observed) · **NUMERICAL
OBSERVATION** (measured on a frozen case) · **EMPIRICAL TREND** (fitted, with a
confidence interval) · **HYPOTHESIS** (not yet tested).

Nothing here may be promoted without the experiment named in the row.

## Relationship to the frozen TX3 envelope

This is a **new research line**, not a continuation of TX3. The frozen envelope
`reports/papers/tx3_connected_intervention_calculus/TX3_FINAL_CLAIM_FREEZE.md`
(2026-08-27) states, for the canonical matched IEEE-39 / three-GFL benchmark:

- **C1, C2, D1–D3 supported** — finite re-equilibrated externalities exist and
  the connected calculus reconstructs them exactly.
- **C3a, C3b REJECTED** — no material effect on the margin-setting mode.
- **C3c UNRESOLVED** — the weak-grid case was baseline-ineligible.
- **N4** — the cycle/SCC localization claim is **UNASSESSED**; E06 was never run.

Two consequences bind this project:

1. No number, figure or wording from TX3 may be reused to support a cycle,
   SCC, provenance or repair claim here. TX3 measured non-additivity and found
   it immaterial on the critical mode; this project asks a different question
   at a frozen operating point.
2. Any IEEE-39 result produced here must be reported alongside the TX3 negative
   result, not instead of it.
