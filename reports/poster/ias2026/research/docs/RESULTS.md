## R2 — Both cases show minimum destabilizing order three (E00, E01)

At the frozen amplitudes, every single action and every pair leaves the system
stable and the full portfolio does not.

| | toy | IEEE-9 |
|---|---|---|
| worst pair | `-0.020165` (AC) | `-0.008014` (AB) |
| full portfolio | `+0.046781` | `+0.003217` |
| created mode | `2.212347 rad/s` | `8.530945 rad/s`, `1.3577 Hz` |
| `kappa` | 3 | 3 |

Cross-checked by the argument principle on `Re(s) > -0.002`: every proper subset
gives winding 0, the full portfolio gives `+2` (the conjugate pair; the count is
per eigenvalue).

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION**, these amplitude vectors and operating points
only. `kappa` is a property of the amplitudes, not of either system.

## R3 — The two cases reach `kappa = 3` by different mechanisms (E00, E01)

Moebius decomposition of the spectral abscissa, and the reconstruction truncated
at each interaction order:

| | order 1 | order 2 | exact | irreducible 3rd | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| toy | `-0.064298` stable | `-0.023223` stable | `+0.046781` **unstable** | `+0.070005` | genuine third order |
| IEEE-9 | `-0.013933` stable | `+0.004023` **unstable** | `+0.003217` unstable | `-0.000806` | pairwise accumulation |

**This is the most important result of the gate phase, and it cuts against the
intended poster narrative.** On the toy the irreducible third-order term is what
crosses the axis: a pairwise-complete model predicts a stable portfolio. On the
realistic IEEE-9 case it is the opposite: the pair term BC (`+0.018913`)
dominates, the genuine triple term is an order of magnitude smaller *and
stabilizing*, and a pairwise-complete model already predicts instability.

So `kappa = 3` on IEEE-9 does **not** demonstrate an irreducible third-order
effect. It demonstrates that lower-order interactions accumulate and only cross
the boundary at the triple. Both are real non-additivity; they are not the same
claim, and the poster may not conflate them.

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION**. `E01` emits `mechanism_verdict` so this is
decided by the data on every future case rather than by narrative.

## R5 — The provenance split is contour dependent, and the guard is admissibility (E00)

On the toy, a circle of radius 0.30 around the created mode encloses the
single-action mode of A and the split flips to individual `+1`, collective `0`.
Both answers are arithmetically correct. `check_admissibility` requires that the
region contain a portfolio mode and no base or lower-order mode, and
`Provenance.verdict` returns `INADMISSIBLE_REGION` rather than a label.

On IEEE-9 the same failure appears at region scale: `Re(s) > -0.05` gives
individual `+2`, collective `0`, because that region already contains the
single-action mode of A at `-0.0275` and the AB and AC modes.

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION**, and a hard requirement on how any provenance
figure is captioned.

## R6 — Winding needs a sample step tied to the clearance, not to a raster (E01)

With a uniform 4000-point D-contour at `Re(s) > -0.002`, every IEEE-9 portfolio
including the unstable one returns winding `0`. The contour passes `0.005` from
the created mode; the phase excursion is complete and cancels between samples
`2.0` apart. Adaptive refinement keyed on the phase increment does not fire,
because the increment it sees is small.

Setting `max_segment = clearance/4` recovers the correct counts (ABC `+2`, every
proper subset `0`) at `1.5e5` evaluations per portfolio. `Winding.resolved_for`
reports whether the step was adequate.

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION** about the method. A winding reported without a
clearance-tied step is not evidence.

## R7 — The controller self-energy of the IEEE-9 case has no simple-pole expansion (E01)

The hidden block is defective: `cond(V) = 1.2e19`, with a fourfold eigenvalue at
the origin from the cascaded integrators (`x_p` feeds `x_id`, `x_q` feeds
`x_iq`). `expand_self_energy` returns `DEFECTIVE_HIDDEN_BLOCK` instead of a
residue table. Section 1.1 of the specification asks for a pole/residue
contribution per controller pole; for this partition that object does not exist.
A Jordan-aware expansion, or a different retained/hidden split, would be needed.

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION**, unresolved. Not a blocker for E00/E01.

## R8 — For this action set the operating point does not move (E01)

Maximum drift of `(x*, z*)` under any single action or the full portfolio:
`9.1e-14`. All three actions scale a quantity that vanishes at equilibrium
(speed deviation, power error, `v_q`). The reduced matrix is additive in the
actions to `2.3e-17`.

Consequence: the frozen-operating-point and re-equilibrated readings of
`METHODS.md` §10 **coincide** for this action set, so the low-rank calculus costs
nothing in physical fidelity here. That is a property of these three actions. A
topology action or a setpoint action would move the power flow and the two
readings would separate.

Status: **NUMERICAL OBSERVATION**, measured rather than assumed.
