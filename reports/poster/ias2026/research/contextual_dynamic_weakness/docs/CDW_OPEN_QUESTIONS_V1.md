# CDW open questions V1 (unresolved scientific questions only; no conclusions)

1. Does the same replacement change the sign of its effect on α_⊥ across legitimate portfolios, and how often across policies?
2. When it does, is it the same modal mechanism reversing, or a switch of the rightmost mode?
3. Is there any fixed node ranking that is adequate for next-replacement decisions, and how much regret does the best one incur?
4. Does contextuality persist when parameter uncertainty changes the minimal failing set?
5. Is α_⊥ submodular, supermodular or neither on the portfolio lattice? Is a tracked-mode margin more structured?
6. For which coordinates does re-equilibration change the sign or the rank of a boundary sensitivity, and under which equilibrium semantics (SPR or RP)?
7. Do total dynamic link sensitivities predict held-out finite interventions better than strong static baselines? Which static baseline is strongest?
8. Do corridors defined only by network structure have a reproducible dynamic ranking? Are finite corridor effects additive over their branches?
9. Can a single admissible topology action remove, or create, an incompatibility? Do static topology scores anticipate it?
10. How much branch strengthening is locally equivalent to a control retuning, and does that equivalence survive finite steps?
11. Does single-boundary tuning leave unsafe intermediate subsets that plan-level design avoids? When do local conflict witnesses appear?
12. Does a context-averaged attribution (Shapley value) hide large contextual variance?
13. Do ordinary static graph scores (Fiedler, effective resistance) predict dynamic or contextual quantities at all?
14. Does the controller policy change graph-modal mixing of the exact port operator at fixed network? Is mixing related to contextuality or to witness transitions?
15. Is there a genuinely reduced model that preserves H, κ and intervention rankings with useful speedup? Can its decisions be certified, or must it abstain?
16. How often is the most energetic observed mode not the stability-limiting mode?
17. Which findings transfer to a different converter model?
18. Is the exact Africano material available? If so, does static PV weak-node ranking capture coalition and dynamic hosting behaviour?
19. Can an improvement of α_⊥ by plan-level design shrink the nonlinear recovery margin near subcritical Hopf boundaries?
