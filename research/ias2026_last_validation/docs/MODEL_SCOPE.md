# Model Scope

The frozen flagship is a phasor-domain DAE with exact rotational symmetry and, without primary restoration, a common-frequency drift partner. Stability claims are transverse/relative claims after deflation of that center structure.

The benchmark does not include converter current limits, DC-link dynamics, modulation delay, ride-through logic, EMT switching, or a physical switching transient between stationary configurations. TDS evidence is nonlinear phasor-domain evidence within the declared voltage/PSS guard envelope.

The documented TGOV1N governor result is a separate policy/model condition. It is evidence of policy dependence, not a contradiction to the no-governor flagship.

IEEE-68 and Kundur are documented secondary benchmarks. ANDES independently checks IEEE-39 network/power-flow/base-mode/governor direction but does not implement the frozen L0 GFL model.
