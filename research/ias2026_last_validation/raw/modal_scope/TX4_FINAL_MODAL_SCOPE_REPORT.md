# TX4 final modal-scope correction report

## Executive verdict

This is a correction and scope audit of the frozen TX4 blind-prediction
campaign, not a new theory or optimization campaign. Four corrections matter:

1. `det(I+Q_H)=0` corresponds to `-1` in the spectrum of `Q_H`.
2. The contextual return `R_i|R=Q_iR(I+Q_RR)^-1Q_Ri` reaches `+1`, not `-1`.
3. Physical local factors are `I+M_ii` before normalization. Diagonal blocks
   of normalized `I+Q` are identity blocks and are not physical evidence.
4. The archived V9 global table mixes an FC10/default policy with the P4
   policy used by the predictor. A same-policy recomputation is therefore
   reported separately and is authoritative for this audit.

The corrected finite evidence supports `FINAL CASE: A`: the V4 collective
mechanism survives the sign and physical-local audit, and the targeted
0.3--1.5 Hz task is much cleaner than the global task. It does not support a
universal V9 safety claim. The IAS poster is ready only with corrected scope
wording. The 6-page paper and TPWRS submission are not ready.

## 2. Sign convention and closure identity

With `C_H=I+Q_H`,

```text
det(I+Q_H)=0  =>  -1 is in sigma(Q_H).
```

For `H={i} union R`,

```text
R_i|R = Q_iR (I+Q_RR)^-1 Q_Ri
det(I+Q_H) = det(I+Q_RR) det(I-R_i|R)
det(I-R_i|R)=0  =>  +1 is in sigma(R_i|R).
```

At the archived full H4 boundary, the fresh audit gives a nearest `Q_H`
distance to `-1` of `3.51e-8`. The four contextual-return distances to `+1`
are between `1.06e-7` and `2.28e-7`; the maximum Schur residual is
`3.63e-16`. The signs are opposite because the two unit conditions belong to
different operators.

## 3. Physical local versus collective factor

The old local table was based on normalized `I+Q` diagonal blocks. Those are
identity by construction and cannot establish physical local regularity. The
correct audit reconstructs the pre-normalized physical action matrix
`M(s)=D(s)K(s)` and evaluates `L_i=I+M_ii`.

Across the frozen controller-gain sweep, the smallest physical local singular
value is `0.2973269`; near the boundary the four local values remain about
`0.4892`, `0.5106`, `0.6573`, and `0.7275`. In contrast, the collective
`sigma_min(I+Q_H)` reaches `1.26e-8`. The minimum proper-subset collective
factor at the boundary frequency is `0.2945461`. This is a finite separation
between local physical regularity and collective closure loss.
