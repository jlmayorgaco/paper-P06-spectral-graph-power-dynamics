# Final-closure preregistration record

The campaign preserves the preregistered tolerances and frozen inputs copied
from the corrective campaign. New claims are not back-filled after observing
the results.

- Canonical P1 tolerances and source hashes: `prereg/P1_NETWORK_TOLERANCE.md`
  and `raw/canonical/canonical_source_manifest.json`.
- Genuine holdout inputs and protocol: `raw/holdout/p6_genuine_holdout_inputs.csv`
  and `raw/holdout/P6_GENUINE_HOLDOUT_PROTOCOL.md`.
- Frozen predictions and digest: `prereg/p6_genuine_predictions.json` and
  `raw/p6/p6_genuine_prediction_sha256.txt`.
- Alternative-model specifications: `docs/SECOND_MODEL_SPEC.md` and the raw
  P2/P5 files.

Because the same-model gate stopped, no mechanism, robustness, or scaling
result is retroactively assigned a positive preregistered status.
