# Robustness and Uncertainty Report

No new physical robust radius, common uncertainty envelope, V4 robust census,
or `g x epsilon` atlas was completed in this closure campaign. These gates are
`NOT_TESTED` and must not be inferred from the frozen nominal tables.

The historical closure supports a narrower statement: the nominal P4 H4
blocker is reproducible, policy dependent, and changes sign under the
documented governor configuration. The execution plan's stronger language
about robustness across an admissible physical uncertainty set is therefore
not promoted.

The uncertainty-weight manifest is retained in `prereg/` as a declaration of
what would be needed for a future preregistered robustness campaign, not as a
result.
