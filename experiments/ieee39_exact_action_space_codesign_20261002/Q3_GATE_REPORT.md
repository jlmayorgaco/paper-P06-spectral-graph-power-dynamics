# Q3 gate report — DDE stability oracle

**Gate result: INDETERMINATE at 20 ms and 40 ms.** The zero-delay system reduces to the finite ODE and has zero eigenvalues in the half-plane `Re(s) > -0.05 s^-1`. For positive delays, a Float64 argument-principle implementation returned winding indices 64 (20 ms) and 62 (40 ms). These numbers are retained in `TABLE_Q03_CONTOUR_ATTEMPT.csv` as diagnostics only. They are not accepted as root counts.

The contour uses a finite radius derived from the resolvent-tail inequality after diagonal similarity balancing; this removes the arbitrary-frequency-cutoff problem. It does not remove floating-point uncertainty. The computation has no interval/rounding-directed enclosure, and independent nonlinear-eigenvalue refinement did not validate the winding indices. In particular, local continuation of the 12 dominant ODE eigenvalue branches found no tracked branch crossing the requested margin, while seeded root attempts in badly scaled coordinates produced false convergence according to a normalized residual and failed refinement after balancing. Neither observation resolves the discrepancy or proves the absence/presence of other DDE roots.

The required intentionally destabilized-gain and near-boundary DDE test cases were not evaluated: the baseline positive-delay oracle did not earn a validated root count, so those comparisons could not be interpreted reliably. `FIG_Q01_REDUCED_NYQUIST.png` and `TABLE_Q03_REDUCED_NYQUIST_DIAGNOSTIC.csv` show the sampled locus, not a validated winding count.

Accordingly, the final `TABLE_Q03_DDE_ROOT_COUNTS.csv` places the unvalidated values in `unverified_winding_index`, leaves `roots_right_of_margin` empty for positive delay, and assigns `INDETERMINATE`. The preregistered decision rule blocks Q7/Q8 until this gate passes. No 20/40 ms optimization, delayed nonlinear event simulation, three-point frontier, or heterogeneous-delay sweep was run. The numerical contour count is a method-development finding, not evidence of stability or instability.
