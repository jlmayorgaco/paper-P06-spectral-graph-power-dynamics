2. Node 38 versus node 34: a different, reduced R1 experiment
-----------------------------------------------------------
PDF source: doc2 p. 2 and pp. 28–35; reproduction contract pp. 40, 45–46.

At the R1 nine-GFL baseline, alpha=+0.091025 s^-1. Raising the PLL natural
frequency by 10% at bus 38 gives alpha=-0.061419 s^-1, while the same change at
bus 34 gives +0.191664 s^-1 (p. 32). The fixed-damping-ratio parameterization
is Kp multiplied by r and Ki multiplied by r^2, with zeta_PLL=0.8. It is not
a Kp-only perturbation. The reported log-frequency sensitivities are -1.243884
at bus 38 and +1.143242 at bus 34. The bus-38 action is the least |log(r)| among
36 enumerated one-bus actions meeting alpha<-0.05; no continuous/global optimum
was established by that enumeration.

R1 is not TX4/GFL11. It uses constant-magnitude SG internal sources, swing and
first-order governor dynamics, normalized PLL and complex-current lag GFLs,
constant-PQ loads, and a finite SG at bus 39. Its relative-frame state count is
3*n_SG-1+4*n_GFL: 29 all-SG, 33 for H4, and 38 for nine GFL plus one SG
(doc2 pp. 28–29). A first-order current lag is not a pure digital time delay.

Doc2 p. 35 reports that all 16 H4 portfolios are stable in R1 at PLL 5 Hz,
also in its 2-Hz and 12-Hz censuses. The margin rises from 0.0811384 to
0.1043014 s^-1 from empty set to H4. This is explicit model dependence,
not a contradiction of the 86-state TX4 result. Its fourth-order Möbius value
1.29249e-4 does not establish a dominant fourth-order mechanism. Its global
margin context reversal need not represent reversal of the same tracked mode.

Other R1 claims belong to this same unlocated source package: re-equilibrated
branch strengthening 26–29 versus 8–9 (p. 34), and 100-MW availability/reference
loss, 12-s linear response, worst-frequency bus 31 versus worst 100-ms RoCoF
bus 34 (pp. 35–37). The event is not a physical converter trip. These bus rankings
must not be merged with the current delayed-model window-frequency rankings.

The PDF advertises code/explore.py, campaign.py, design_extension.py,
structural.py, control_actions.py, figures_tables.py; load_step_ranking.csv;
metadata.json and a SHA-256 package manifest. Those named campaign scripts and
CSV were not found by targeted repository filename searches, and their names
were absent from the inspected deliverables ZIP inventories. The repository's
code directory instead contains the TX4 package. Therefore the PDF's numbers
are attributable and internally interpretable but not independently reproducible
from the located R1 artifacts here. Obtain the exact R1 package before elevating
these results into a repository-backed headline or regenerating its figures.

3. 43.9% Fiedler improvement / damping 5.152 -> 2.876 -> 5.326
----------------------------------------------------------------
PDF source: doc5 abstract p. 1, Table V and sections E–F p. 22, Fig. 9 p. 23.

The 43.9% is (5.117/3.556-1)*100 from the retained-port Fiedler values of a
closed versus radial single-POI collector. Damping falls from 5.152% to 2.876%
(44.2% relative loss). The recommended radial two-POI (25,26) case has 5.326%
minimum damping and Fiedler 8.549. Table V's other radial two-POI (25,29)
case has 5.327%; those nearby numbers refer to different topologies, not a typo.

This is an IEEE-39 PLUS three-project cluster: new buses 40–42 at 345 kV inject
95 MW each, totaling 285 MW. Every topology gets a new nonlinear AC solution;
the full P-Q injection Jacobian is formed; constant-PQ load buses are eliminated;
angle/frequency/voltage/PLL/reactive-control states are retained. Nominal reduced
GFL gains are Kp=13 and Ki=400. These are declared transparent surrogates,
not manufacturer models. It is an added-project connection study, not an
SG-to-GFL replacement-fraction study and not the TX4 H4 experiment.

All six Table V cases are stated to be AC feasible and asymptotically stable.
The relevant failure is the selected 5% damping screen. The loop-closure sweep
crosses that screen near 0.543; the reduced cycle-return magnitude becomes
order one near 0.488. Neither event makes rho(H+)=1 a universal stability
threshold. A single-POI radial/closed mode comparison is 7.98/7.51 Hz, whereas
the radial two-POI design's limiting mode is 2.64 Hz. Do not describe the full
5.152 -> 2.876 -> 5.326 sequence as one continuously tracked eigenpair unless
the absent tracking data establish that identity.

The PDF preserves important negative results. For its 10-MW, 100-ms pulse at
bus 40, closed versus recommended radial (25,26) frequency deviation is
0.152 versus 0.175 Hz, PLL angle error 1.93 versus 2.36 degrees, and voltage
deviation 1.44e-3 versus 7.21e-4 pu. Better damping does not improve every
reported transient output. Gmax remains near eight; the paper expressly does
not demonstrate a non-normal Braess effect (p. 22).

The source code, six-topology CSV, 31x33 closure/Kp sweep, AC operating points,
localization records, and pulse traces for this package were not located.
Targeted searches covered collector/three_gfl/cluster filenames and the
characteristic numerical claims in textual artifacts; deliverables ZIP member
lists also lacked the matching campaign identifiers. Appendix M (p. 28) states
what a submission-ready implementation SHOULD archive, rather than identifying
a deposited runnable artifact. Thus these are PDF-only measurements for this
audit. Do not reconstruct raw data from the PDF table and present it as a
reproduced simulation.

Other doc5 numbers share that provenance status, including its 17,664 and
23,000 action-pair scans (p. 21), 1,023 collector cases, and connected-calculus
random identity checks (p. 20). Their explicit counts are not additional
independent confirmations when all originate in this one unlocated package.
The controller-frequency example on p. 21 is 5.1 -> 4.8 rad/s, not Hz.

5. Doc4 is robotics: useful discipline, no power-system validation
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Doc4 p. 1 identifies a TFM robotics/automation audit of
MROB_Integracion_v3/src/cargo_cl.py, with 199 snapshots from ten existing worlds.
It is not a power-system experiment. Useful transferable ideas are separating
equilibrium, stability, physical feasibility, safety, and mission performance;
distinguishing an algebraic infeasibility witness from a solver failure; testing
the actually sampled controller; recognizing loss of control authority; and
requiring a true lower bound before claiming global optimality (pp. 3, 14–15).
Its 199 correlated snapshots are not 199 independent trials. Do not transplant
robotics numerical certificates, constants, or empirical outcomes into the
power-system evidence chain. Its monotonicity cautions are conceptual analogies.

6. Recommended strongest existing story and evidence requirements
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use the exact, finite H4 compatibility failure as the empirical anchor, with the
physical collective closure as its model-scoped explanation. State the model,
policy, state count, same-model Python/Julia agreement, and all 16 subset labels
together. Preserve the 110 global false-safe cases as a limit on selected-mode
prediction, rather than suppressing them. This yields a useful engineering
message: compatibility is contextual, and a targeted modal explanation is not
an all-mode safety certificate.

Use doc2 to explain why nominally identical network benchmarks and retuning
actions can give different answers under different device/load models. Its
node-placement example becomes a stronger design result only once its own
code/data/parameters are recovered. The R1 all-stable H4 result is informative
transfer evidence, not a reason to silently choose whichever model is favorable.

Use the existing TX3 package for the supported finite-interaction theory, while
retaining its negative operational-materiality result. The proposed connected
calculus can organize a new test, but theoretical decomposition, a large proxy,
and a successful intervention are three separate evidence claims.

The doc5 collector story is an attractive candidate application, but currently
has the weakest provenance among these numerical anchors. Recover and verify
its exact model, AC equilibria, branch definitions/units, six raw spectra,
mode-tracking records, full 1,023-case sweep, and pulse outputs before claiming
it as reproduced evidence. Keep additions of new GFL plants, whole-machine
replacement, and the current fractional delayed-PLL model in separate tables.
Neither these PDFs nor these artifact checks establish a maximum replacement
fraction, a gain-independent global barrier, an EMT validation, or a globally
optimal design.

For the current network-moment experiment, use its own export/model manifest
and independent review rather than importing model parameters or metrics from
R1/TX4/doc5. The present review did not rerun or assess that newer experiment's
latest nonlinear outputs. A lower-frequency moment identity is also not, by
itself, a broadband transient-performance or all-mode stability guarantee.
