PRIMARY-SOURCE COMPARISON — searched/read 2026-10-03

Peaucelle & Ebihara (IFAC 2014)
Robust stability analysis of discrete-time systems with parametric and switching uncertainties
https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/ifac2014/media/files/0282.pdf
Overlap: retaining algebraic/auxiliary variables for rational parameter dependence.
Boundary: the new article does not claim invention of affine descriptor lifting.

Bindel & Hood (2013)
Localization Theorems for Nonlinear Eigenvalue Problems
https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~bindel/papers/2013-simax.pdf
Overlap: contour root count and perturbation homotopy with multiplicities.
Boundary: internal collisions and norm-based contour preservation are established.

Beyn (2010 author version)
An integral method for solving nonlinear eigenvalue problems
https://arxiv.org/abs/1003.1580
Overlap: resolvent contour moments for nonlinear eigenvalues.
Boundary: the application here is a finite parameter-region necessary replacement
relaxation; generic use of contour moments is not a novelty claim.

Borgioli, Michiels, Lu & Vandereycken (2019)
A globally convergent method to compute the real stability radius for time-delay systems
https://www.unige.ch/math/vandereycken/papers/published_Borgioli_MLV.pdf
Overlap: fixed delays, structured real perturbations, determinant return matrices.
Difference: distance to destabilizing uncertainty versus maximizing beneficial
replacement with bounded adjustable PLL gains. Their global guarantee does not
transfer automatically to this repeated-diagonal co-design domain.

Michiels & Gumussoy (2014; arXiv author version 2020)
Eigenvalue based algorithms and software for the design of fixed-order
stabilizing controllers for interconnected systems with time-delays
https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.05496
Overlap: structured controller optimization for delayed descriptor systems.
Role: the strongest algorithmic family for a fair same-contract comparator.
No head-to-head experiment with this family has been executed here.

Markovic et al. (TPWRS 2021)
Understanding Small-Signal Stability of Low-Inertia Systems
https://sps.cut.ac.cy/publication/2021jmarkovicb/2021JMarkovicb.pdf
DOI 10.1109/TPWRS.2021.3061434
Overlap: detailed machine/converter dynamics, collective modes, PLL settings and
permissible converter integration. Their PLL-gain/penetration illustration is
not to be relabeled as the same SG-to-GFL replacement contract.

Chu & Teng (arXiv author version 2023)
Stability Constrained Optimization in High IBR-Penetrated Power Systems—Part I
https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12151
Overlap: incorporating synchronization, voltage and frequency limits in optimization.
Difference: the present model retains the delayed characteristic operator;
gSCR constraints have another model/guarantee scope. No numerical superiority
against their method is established.

Jia et al. (TPWRS 2025)
Converter-Driven Stability Constrained Generation Expansion for High IBG-Penetrated Power Systems
https://doi.org/10.1109/TPWRS.2025.3558879
Closest application overlap: converter-driven modal stability across generation
mixtures. Only publication metadata and abstract-level methodology were available
to the review; no detailed implementation comparison is claimed.

Johansson (IEEE Transactions on Computers 2017)
Arb: Efficient Arbitrary-Precision Midpoint-Radius Interval Arithmetic
https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.02831
https://doi.org/10.1109/TC.2017.2690633
Role: verified real/complex ball arithmetic used for the continuous contour gate.

VERDICT
No exact duplicate of the full model-specific construction was found in this
limited search. This does not establish first publication. The useful novelty
must be demonstrated by a nonvacuous replacement decision, not by renaming
standard mathematics or by a better intermediate norm alone.
