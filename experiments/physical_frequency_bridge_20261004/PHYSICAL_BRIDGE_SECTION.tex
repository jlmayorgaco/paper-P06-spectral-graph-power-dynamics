\section{A physical-energy bridge to the detailed IEEE--39 model}
\label{sec:physical-energy-bridge}

The obstruction in Section~\ref{sec:nonlinear-fixed-bus} has a declared
lossless swing contract. Before using it to constrain converter replacement
in the detailed network, we audit the energy ports of that network. This
section establishes an exact model identity and a restricted numerical
bridge; it does not identify a new maximum-replacement design.

\subsection{DC power orientation and a spectrally invisible distinction}
Write $p_a$ for converter power delivered to the AC filter and $p_d$ for
power supplied at its DC port. The frozen implementation uses
\begin{align}
 C\dot v_o&=(p_a-p_d)/v_o,&
 i_{d,o}^{\rm ref}&=k_v(V-v_o)+z_o,&
 \dot z_o&=k_{iv}(V-v_o).\label{eq:legacy-dc-bridge}
\end{align}
For the positive-energy variant constructed in a new experiment directory,
the three corresponding equations are
\begin{align}
 C\dot v&=(p_d-p_a)/v,&
 i_d^{\rm ref}&=k_v(v-V)+z,&
 \dot z&=k_{iv}(v-V).\label{eq:physical-dc-bridge}
\end{align}
Here $V>0$ is constant. The remaining current, PLL, SG and network equations
are unchanged. In this implementation $v$ does not otherwise enter a
modulation or protection block. Both models have the same trim
$v_o=v=V$, $p_a=p_d$ and the same integral-controller state.

\begin{proposition}[Tangent equivalence at the declared trim]
Let $S$ reflect only the deviation of each DC-voltage state, and act as the
identity on every other differential and algebraic coordinate. At an exact
common trim, with corresponding equation order, the descriptor matrices obey
\begin{equation}
 E_p=SE_oS,\qquad A_p=SA_oS.\label{eq:dc-similarity-bridge}
\end{equation}
For perturbations of the unchanged AC/DC power ports, $B_p=SB_o$; for AC
outputs independent of the DC-voltage coordinate, $C_p=C_oS$, with unchanged
feedthrough. Hence the linear AC transfer functions and finite spectra agree.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $\widetilde v=-\delta v_o$, $\widetilde z=\delta z_o$. The two PI
equations have identical first variations under this substitution. Since
$p_a-p_d=0$ at trim, differentiation of the denominator adds no first-order
term; the DC power row changes sign exactly. Every remaining row is unchanged
after the same state substitution. This proves the descriptor and port
identities. Algebraic elimination preserves the similarity wherever its
eliminated block is nonsingular.
\end{proof}
This is classical similarity algebra applied to the actual port equations,
not a new general spectral theorem. The nonlinear reflection
$v_o=2V-v$ fails in general: its DC-row defect is proportional to
\begin{equation}
 \frac{p_d-p_a}{C}\left(\frac1v-\frac1{2V-v}\right),
\end{equation}
which is second order near trim. Limits, modulation dependence on $v$, or
perturbing the voltage reference require a new analysis. Identical poles
therefore do not establish identical finite-disturbance security. An
identically inserted PLL measurement delay would preserve the corresponding
linear characteristic-operator similarity; no nonlinear DDE simulation is
claimed by the present zero-delay campaign.

\subsection{Positive storage and an exact finite-time frequency identity}
Use $s_i=100\rho_i$ MVA for the aggregate converter scale, and the declared
per-unit filter and capacitor equations. The accounted storage is
\begin{align}
 E&=K+E_f+E_{dc},\nonumber\\
 K&=\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}H_iS_{n,i}\omega_i^2,\nonumber\\
 E_f&=\sum_i\frac{s_iX_{f,i}}{2\omega_b}\|i_{f,i}\|^2,
 &E_{dc}&=\sum_i\frac{s_iC_i}{2}v_i^2.
 \label{eq:bridge-storage}
\end{align}
The filter equations cancel their rotational cross terms and give
$\dot E_f=P_a-P_{\rm GFL}-P_{\rm loss,f}$, while
\eqref{eq:physical-dc-bridge} gives $\dot E_{dc}=P_d-P_a$.
The SG swing and algebraic stator equations give
$\dot K=\sum_i P_{m,i}-P_{\rm SG}-P_{\rm Cu}
-\sum_iS_{n,i}D_i\omega_i(\omega_i-1)$.
Network active-power balance then proves
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\dot E=\sum_iP_{m,i}+P_{d,\rm total}-\mathcal L(t)},
 \label{eq:bridge-energy}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal L$ includes realized load, network/filter/copper losses,
and the displayed machine-damping term. In this frozen SG parameter set,
$R_s=D=0$. The storage is positive in the declared state variables; this
does not make it a deviation Lyapunov function or certify passivity, DC-source
energy availability, or omitted electromagnetic storage.

An exact finite-time identity follows without assuming a final equilibrium.
Set $\nu_i=\omega_i-1$, $I_i(T)=\int_0^T\nu_i(t)\,dt$, and let $\mathcal G$
contain retained SGs with TGOV1. Their implemented equations satisfy
\begin{align}
 T_{1i}\dot x_{1i}&=r_i-x_{1i}+a_i,&
 T_{3i}\dot x_{2i}&=x_{1i}+T_{2i}\dot x_{1i}-x_{2i},\nonumber\\
 r_i&=(p_{{\rm ref},i}-\nu_i)/R_i,&
 P_{m,i}&=S_{n,i}(x_{2i}-D_{T,i}\nu_i).
\end{align}
The actual limiter supplies $a_i=0$ in the interior and
$a_i=x_{1i}-r_i$ when it freezes $\dot x_{1i}$. For fixed references and
an initial trim, define
\begin{align}
 d_i&=S_{n,i}(R_i^{-1}+D_{T,i}),\nonumber\\
 B_g(T)&=\sum_{i\in\mathcal G}S_{n,i}
 [(T_{2i}-T_{1i})\Delta x_{1i}-T_{3i}\Delta x_{2i}],\nonumber\\
 A_{aw}(T)&=\sum_{i\in\mathcal G}S_{n,i}
 \int_0^T[a_i(t)-a_i(0)]\,dt.
\end{align}
The remaining retained SG at bus39 has fixed torque, so
$\Delta P_{m,39}=P_{m,39}(0)\nu_{39}$ exactly. Integrating the two governor
equations and \eqref{eq:bridge-energy} yields
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{i\in\mathcal G}d_iI_i-P_{m,39}(0)I_{39}
 &=B_g+A_{aw}-\Delta E\\
 &\quad+\int_0^T\Delta P_{d,\rm total}\,dt
       -\int_0^T\Delta\mathcal L\,dt .
 \end{aligned}}\label{eq:bridge-finite-area}
\end{equation}
The bus39 term is omitted if that SG is absent. A nonzero initial power
residual $b_0$ adds $Tb_0$ on the right. No nonlinear mechanical-power product
has been dropped: the implemented TGOV1 output is torque times speed.
This identity uses rotor frequency, not the PLL state or the windowed bus
phase estimator. The actual ZIP consumption and limiter branch must be used.

\subsection{What is still required for a predictive replacement bound}
For a convergent full-model trajectory, a conditional infinite-horizon form
is also possible. Suppose the balance is represented as
$\dot E+\sum_i(h_i*\nu_i)=P+r(t)$, with fixed causal power-response kernels,
finite first moments, and integrable deviations from the final synchronized
frequency $\Omega$. Require $E\to E_\infty$, $r-r_\infty\in L^1$,
zero perturbation prehistory, and integrable frequency deviations.
Write $H_i(s)$ for the Laplace transform of $h_i$.
Let $d_i=H_i(0)$ denote DC transfer gains,
$d_0=\sum_i d_i>0$, $\kappa=\sum_i H_i'(0)$ denote the sum of first Laplace
coefficients, and $\mathcal R=\int_0^\infty(r-r_\infty)dt$.
For consistent phase endpoints $\delta_i$ in coordinates
$q_i=\theta_i/\omega_b$, relative to the nominal rotating frame, and
output $j$, define
$\eta_i=\lim_{t\to\infty}[q_i(t)-q_i(0)-\Omega t]$ and
$\delta_i-\delta_j=\eta_i-\eta_j$. These are changes of relative phase
from the initial trim, not absolute final phase differences. Integration gives
\begin{equation}
 d_0\mathcal A_j=\Delta E+\kappa\Omega
       +\sum_i d_i(\delta_i-\delta_j)-\mathcal R .
 \label{eq:bridge-defect-area}
\end{equation}
Here $\mathcal A_j=\int_0^\infty(\Omega-\nu_j)dt$. Algebraic phase jumps
must be included distributionally or through the declared phase estimator.
For a causal rectangular estimator of width $W$ with the proper prehistory,
$\mathcal A_{j,W}=\mathcal A_j+\Omega W/2$.
For synchronized rotors and $m=2\sum_{i\in\mathcal S}H_iS_{n,i}$,
$\Delta K=m\Omega+(m/2)\Omega^2$, not merely $m\Omega$.
The torque-fixed SG can give a negative $d_i$.

Equation~\eqref{eq:bridge-defect-area} separates the missing predictive
ingredients: full-model phase endpoints, storage change, and a uniform bound
on $\mathcal R$ over the admissible design family. ZIP consumption, losses
and limiter engagement depend on the controller trajectory. A residual
measured after a run is an audit, not such a uniform bound. Consequently,
the lossless effective-resistance floor cannot yet be asserted for this
IEEE--39 replacement contract. PLL retuning changes collective trajectories
and governor engagement; it adds no independent sustained active-power
droop source to the present converter equations.
