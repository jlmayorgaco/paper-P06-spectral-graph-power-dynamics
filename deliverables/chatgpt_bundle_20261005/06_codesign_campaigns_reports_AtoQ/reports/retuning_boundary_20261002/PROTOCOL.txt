IEEE39 RETUNING BOUNDARY — exploratory campaign, frozen initial scope 2026-10-02.

Scientific question: distinguish finite SG-to-GFL substitutions recoverable by
PLL retuning from operating constraints invariant to those gains.

Theory remains parameterized by network, disturbance class, observation/filter
definition, resources and limits. IEEE39 and the following events are instances.
Model: existing physical_supply nonlinear DAE with all interior co-located SG/GFL
aggregates retained. Reuse dependencies; no changes to shared model sources.

Primary exact obstruction to investigate: instantaneous post-event algebraic
voltage/phase at continuous device states, which is independent of PLL gains.
For the existing rectangular-window phase measurements (T=0.5 s), a phase jump
gives F(0+)=jump/(2*pi*T), R(0+)=jump/(2*pi*T^2). These are measured quantities,
not instantaneous rotor acceleration. Voltage bounds also apply at the jump.
This concerns the stated phasor DAE and load-switch model, not an EMT claim.

uniform rho grid .875,.90,.925,.95,.975,.99,.995,.999;
events +/-100 MW changes of Z-load Pset at buses 8,16,29.

finite substitutions and bounded gain retuning, with matched fixed-gain cases.
