# Frozen local retuning protocol

At fixed high replacement 88.45514078456176%, start independently from Design Z and Design N. All 20 nodal gain coordinates are free in log space within the unchanged source gain box. For the first predictor at each start, compare (a) the exact one-active-mode Euclidean-ball support direction with ‖Δlog K‖2≤0.01 and (b) a multimode linear program maximizing the smallest of the two earliest **local exact-characteristic crossing predictions**. The multimode step has |Δlog K_j|≤0.01 and Σ|Δlog K_j|≤0.05, plus physical gain bounds. These are conservative local trust coordinates, not assertions of optimal physical gains.

For each predictor, perform full-model relinearization, rediscover the fast families, and check the complete DDE contour around any proposed new crossing. Require zero-delay α≤−0.05 s⁻¹, equilibrium/guard pass, and all five frozen 61-second nonlinear zero-delay events for an **accepted** candidate. If any condition fails, halve the step and retry; reject it rather than changing the limits. Do not infer delayed nonlinear-event safety. Continue local retuning while a fully validated improvement ≥0.01 ms is found; stop after three successive accepted steps below 0.01 ms or a supported active-set stationarity diagnostic. Without a global bound, call the result *best found*, not globally optimal.

The two-mode LP is a predictor; a third branch must be checked by the exact corrector. At a family crossing the scalar gradient of the minimum margin is generally nondifferentiable. No single-mode gradient may be promoted to a global smooth gradient there.
