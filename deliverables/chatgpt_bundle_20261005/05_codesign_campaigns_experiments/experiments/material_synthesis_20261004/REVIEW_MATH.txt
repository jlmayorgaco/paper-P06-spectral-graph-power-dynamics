INDEPENDENT SKEPTICAL MATHEMATICAL REVIEW
2026-10-04. Scope: extracted attached PDFs, with emphasis on PDF3 and PDF5,
and comparison with the current THEORY and network_moment_codesign sources.
Page numbers below are physical PDF pages, not the Spanish printed page labels.
No simulations were run. No frozen/main source was changed.

BOTTOM-LINE ASSESSMENT

PDF3 has the strongest existing engineering object: a precisely scoped minimal
replacement blocker, its exact contextual return, and a testable physical repair.
PDF1 adds a stronger architectural restriction than another interaction index:
the full-network frequency-moment law. PDF5 offers a useful synthesis, but its
partition/Mobius/holonomy/manifold terminology is mostly classical mathematics,
and several claims exceed the formulas actually proved. The most promising
next result is a certified finite lower bound on the number of moment-neutral
graph-control links needed to repair a forbidden mode, paired with a feasible
upper witness. It is a candidate engineering theorem, not an established result
of the current experiments or a new general small-gain theorem.

1. WHAT CHECKS OUT, AND WHAT IS ALREADY STANDARD

PDF5 pp5-8,26-28: implicit differentiation on a regular AC equilibrium branch,
Jacobi determinant transport, the mixed partition-cycle derivative of log det,
and the cubical integral of a smooth anchored Mobius dividend are correct.
For a set S of distinct action labels, the coefficient
  (-1)^(|pi|-1)/|pi| times sum over all block permutations
has the right multiplicity because trace is cyclic. In the affine case only
singleton blocks remain, yielding distinct-label cyclic products. These are
chain-rule/implicit-function, log-determinant, and finite-difference identities.
Their possible contribution is a decision enabled by a physical model, not the
identities themselves. A gauge-fixed nonsingular equilibrium Jacobian and one
smooth equilibrium branch over the WHOLE action cell are required; a local IFT
at the anchor does not supply this for a finite line outage or controller switch.

PDF5 pp9-10,26: determinant cycle covers, acyclic support sufficiency, SCC
factorization, the isolated block-cycle determinant, and the distinction between
simple cycles and repeated closed walks are sound. The overlap counterexample
1-ab-cd is valuable: neither isolated cycle need close for the whole core to do
so. Physical transmission cycles and action-return cycles are different objects.

PDF5 pp11-12,27: the polynomial inverse-entry estimate is correct under its
diagonalizability/approximation hypotheses, and eta<1 gives the stated
-r log(1-eta) determinant-magnitude error. A spectral gap alone does not imply
geometric polynomial approximation of 1/z on an arbitrary compact set; a compact
set encircling zero supplies the basic obstruction. Nonnormal conditioning can
make locality bounds useless. The paper appropriately makes the geometric
approximation conditional, but no generic grid-locality guarantee follows.

PDF5 pp16,27: the contour perturbation theorem is an application of
Rouche/Gohberg-Sigal, with analytic/meromorphic hypotheses and bookkeeping.
This is established theory; the claimed application is choosing an exterior
model by its port error. Primary sources checked in this review:
  Gohberg-Sigal (1971), DOI 10.1070/SM1971v013n04ABEH003702:
  https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=sm&option_lang=eng&paperid=3169&wshow=paper
  Benzi-Razouk, sparse matrix-function decay (author technical report):
  https://www.cs.emory.edu/technical-reports/techrep-00021.pdf
Sparse pole placement is also established, including minimum-gain placement with
sparsity constraints: https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08762 . These checks are not an
exhaustive novelty or citation audit of the PDFs.

2. MATERIAL GAPS IN PDF5

A. Spectral potential attribution is not generally representation invariant.
   Pages7-8,12-13,15,17-18,21,25.

Let T_tilde(s,a)=exp(h(s,a)) T(s,a), with h analytic in s and smooth in a.
This is an invertible equation normalization: roots and the modeled dynamics do
not change. Nevertheless
  log|det T_tilde| = log|det T| + n Re h,
so a mixed derivative or finite dividend can be changed arbitrarily. Taking
h=c*a_i*a_j, for example, changes the pair dividend by n*c*alpha_i*alpha_j.
Dividing by the baseline or subtracting singleton factors removes separable
normalizations, but not this joint normalization. A fixed physical descriptor
and port normalization can DEFINE a useful convention; it does not make the
number an intrinsic dynamic externality. Regularized logdet(T*T+epsilon^2 I)
also needs physical norms and units; arbitrary nonunitary scalings change it.

The invariant correction is already substantially present in PDF1 pp6-10,16-18:
  S_m(a)=(1/(2*pi*i))*integral_Gamma s^m tr(T^-1 T_s) ds.
If analytic L,R are invertible on AND inside Gamma, replacing T by LTR leaves
every S_m unchanged, because the added logarithmic derivatives are analytic.
On a common nonsingular action cell,
  D_S S_m = -m/(2*pi*i)*integral_Gamma s^(m-1) D_S logdet(T) ds.
This follows by integration by parts; mixed log derivatives are single valued.
For m=0 this derivative is zero: an integer count is locally constant. For a
single enclosed conjugate pair with no poles, S_1=2 Re(lambda). For a larger
cluster, its centroid is only a necessary stability diagnostic, not full safety.
For a Schur pencil, include the weighted moments of eliminated poles as well as
root counts: moving hidden poles change S_m for m>0.

Even after contour integration the separate 'intrinsic' and 'feedback' terms
need not be invariant. Action-dependent row transformations alter T_i and T_ij;
the changes cancel in the sum, not necessarily in each proposed share. Use the
split as a diagnostic of a declared physical representation. This correction
is NOT a new contribution beyond the current PDF1/repository contour theory.

B. The curvature bound does not certify the frozen affine model error.
   Page8 eq(52), pages23-24 Gate A.

The bound controls omitted nonsingleton partitions of the exact mixed derivative
at the actual point a. The retained singleton partition still uses G(a) and
T_i(a). It does not bound replacing them by their baseline values, freezing the
equilibrium, compressing dense total derivatives to local low-rank ports, or
omitting pure-coordinate nonlinearities.
Counterexample: T(a,b)=1+a^2+b on [0,1]^2. T_ab=0 everywhere, so the pair
curvature-vertex bound is zero. The baseline affine tangent is 1+b and has zero
pair interaction, but the true finite log dividend is log(3)-2*log(2), nonzero.
Thus Gate A cannot require eq(52) to cover the full frozen-model error as written.
A useful replacement gate needs a bound on the full operator remainder
T(a)-[T(0)+sum a_i T_i(0)], including pure second derivatives, followed by a
uniform contour perturbation bound (or an exact nonlinear action model).

C. Piecewise smoothness alone does not preserve the stated integral identity.
   Page26 Appendix C; damping-minimum applications pages17,21.

For f(a,b)=min(a,b) on [0,1]^2, Delta_ab f=1, while the classical mixed derivative
f_ab is zero on both open smooth pieces. Its area integral is zero. Interface
or distributional terms are essential. Thus zeta_min, maximum real part, and
protection min/max objectives cannot use the smooth mixed-derivative integral
across mode switches without additional terms or a smooth branch restriction.
Endpoint Mobius inversion remains perfectly valid for any finite set function.
Moreover, such an objective can have a nonzero dividend even for dynamically
disconnected subsystems: switching the active minimum/maximum is an objective
aggregation effect, not a newly discovered feedback interaction.

D. Holonomy scalarization overstates the matrix theorem.
   Pages10-11,26-27, especially Propositions11 and15.

Simultaneous diagonalization of a finite family of diagonalizable matrices on
ONE space is equivalent to pairwise commutation. That statement is correct.
Holonomies at different base vertices may act on different spaces/dimensions.
They cannot simply be put into one algebra or commutator without identification.
A sufficient setting is equal-dimensional fibers, invertible edge transports,
chosen spanning-tree identifications, and all relevant non-tree returns based
at a common vertex. A graph cycle-space basis is not automatically a generating
set for noncommutative directed path products with singular/rectangular edges.
Even with a common space, a reducible algebra need not admit block-DIAGONAL
channels: a 2x2 Jordan block has a proper invariant subspace but no invariant
complement. Invariant flags give block triangularization; direct-sum reduction
requires complementary reducing subspaces/appropriate semisimplicity. Also,
pointwise bases at each frequency need not define one physical frequency-band
controller basis. Preserve the valid isolated-cycle similarity invariants; do
not infer scalar grid-strength adequacy from the present Proposition11 alone.

E. The scalability claim needs uniform support and the right objective.
   Pages15-16,24-25.

An exact fixed affine determinant on block-principal restrictions does factor by
SCC. A pair-cumulant support graph can miss a pure directed three-cycle because
all pair cyclic traces can vanish. Use certified port support, not nonzero pairs
alone. Re-equilibration vertices can couple distinct tangent SCCs. Pruning one
portfolio at one frequency is not a certificate for all coalitions/cell points.
Finally a generic damping minimum is not additive across independent SCCs;
the max/min aggregation can itself couple its coalition dividends. Therefore
sum_c 2^(m_c) is not yet a general exact complexity guarantee for the stated
re-equilibrated physical security game. The p25 'prime-cycle Shapley result'
needs an explicit theorem for the precise determinant/logdet game, convergence
domain, restriction property, and output; it cannot silently transfer to zeta_min.

F. EMT and action-distance qualifications affect the proposed headline.
   Pages13-16,27-28.

Preserving exterior self-energy certifies the stated retained-core linear count,
subject to hidden factors. An unobservable decoupled unstable exterior state can
leave self-energy unchanged. Full-model claims must include the hidden factors
of both models and the relevant contour/high-frequency scope. For external
disturbances or outputs, elimination changes B_eff, C_eff and direct feedthrough
as well as Sigma. The transfer identity on p16 assumes those maps are shared;
Sigma alone does not ensure the required I/O accuracy. The declared nonlinear
and manufacturer-EMT evidence gates remain uncompleted; p28 correctly warns
that a stationary holonomy cannot certify faults or limit switching.

The action-radius lower bound on p14 applies only to failure represented by a
change of the protected contour count, starting from an appropriate safe count.
It is not automatically a bound for the arbitrary waveform/protection unsafe
set used in eq(94). If the Lipschitz bound is known only in a radius-R ball, the
guaranteed radius is at most min(R,delta_Gamma/L_Gamma). A fixed weighted chord
norm is also not a geodesic distance for a state-dependent metric W(a).

The spectral one-form on p15 is locally exact and globally closed; connected
nonsingular does not imply globally exact. Its loop integral may be 2*pi*i*k.
The determinant ratio is endpoint invariant; its transported logarithm can
depend on path winding. The later wording partly acknowledges this distinction.

3. PDF3: RETAIN THE STRONG CLAIM, NOT A UNIVERSAL H4 LAW

Pages3-6: the exact invariant-subspace quotient, physical terminal replacement
factorization, and contextual-return Schur argument are sound for the declared
model. Check the uniform-frequency Jordan identity in the actual full equations;
rotation covariance alone proves the gauge, not the extra Jordan partner.
Frequency-translation equivariance of the entire device model is needed for
that stronger identity. This frozen no-governor model is not the simple-gauge
governed model of PDF1's moment section.

Page4: the blocker antichain is elementary but useful. Its precise planning
meaning is safety of every subset/every sequential stationary order. An endpoint
containing a blocker can nevertheless be stable after additional replacements:
stability is not monotone. It does not imply that every order is unsafe, that
there is no safe continuous retuning path, or that the switching transient is
safe. Those are separate decision problems.

Pages5,8-10: contextual unity return and nonzero local critical vectors follow
from proper-subset regularity. The residue repair derivative is correct and
classical. The log-strength derivative rho*d lambda/d rho is invariant under
unit rescaling rho'=c*rho, not arbitrary nonlinear reparameterization. Its finite
ranking remains an empirical test; the manuscript correctly calls it pending.

Pages7-8,11: preserve the 110 full-spectrum false-safe cases of the EM-band
predictor, the small connected four-way share, and disappearance of the specific
H4 witness when governors are added. Those prevent three unjustified claims:
complete-spectrum safety, irreducible four-body physics, and a bus-intrinsic H4.
PDF2 pp35,40-41 independently reinforces model dependence: its R1 architecture
does not reproduce H4, and its finite event evidence has explicit limitations.

4. ONE RECOMMENDED NEXT THEOREM/DESIGN QUESTION

Can one certify the MINIMUM NUMBER of added graph-control links needed to repair
a latency-induced forbidden mode while preserving the full-network frequency
moments, within a fixed and physically bounded controller architecture?

This extends the conditional graph direction of PDF1 p40. Its new output would
be a finite architecture requirement and feasible repair, not another projector
or an assertion that graph gains outperform diagonal gains. It is not yet known
to be nonvacuous on IEEE39. The following theorem is provable from the existing
exact model and has a focused, falsifiable test.

Assumptions and controller contract.

Fix the plant, SG/GFL replacement rho, equilibrium, integral matrix KI (invertible),
baseline proportional matrix KP0, filter Tf and detector delays
E_tau(s)=diag(exp(-s*tau_i)). The open all-PLL plant gives e=G(s)theta+g(s)d,
with the same analytic hidden/input/output maps for every proposed edge action.
Let B=[b_e] be the incidence matrix of the physically allowed communication
edges, b_e=unit_i-unit_j, so B^T r=0 for the rotational vector r=1.
Declare in advance finite engineering limits |t_e|<=bar_t_e (or 0<=t_e<=bar_t_e)
and allowed supports. The NEW control architecture is
  KP(t)=KP0+KI B diag(t) B^T KI.
It is not the already implemented diagonal-PLL family. Signal access, channel
delays, units and off-diagonal gain costs need their own controller contract.

Near s=0 assume the repository moment hypotheses: regular hidden block, rank
K0=n-1, K0*r=0, ell* K0=0, d0=ell* K1*r nonzero, and analytic physical output
maps. With (s KP+KI) E_tau acting on detector error, the retained controller is
  K(s)=E_tau(s)^(-1) (s KP+KI)^(-1) s^2(I+s Tf)-G(s).
Then K2ctrl=KI^(-1) and
  K3ctrl=T_tau KI^(-1)+KI^(-1)Tf-KI^(-1)KP KI^(-1).
Any FINITE edge update has Delta K3=-B diag(t) B^T and Delta K3*r=0.
The resolvent identity and common rank-one synchronization residue therefore
give Delta H(s)=O(s^3): H0,H1,H2 are identical exactly. No small gain change is
required for coefficient equality. Stable time-domain moment interpretations
require both designs to be stable and the corresponding integrals to converge.
Neither this identity nor edge balance proves modal improvement.

Exact finite edge-return law.

Use the un-inverted-gain retained PLL equation
  J0(s)=s^2(I+s Tf)-(s KP0+KI) E_tau(s) G(s).
It avoids spurious singularities from inverting s KP+KI. The updated operator is
  Jt(s)=J0(s)-s KI B diag(t) B^T KI E_tau(s) G(s).
For s on a chart where J0 is invertible, define
  L(s)=s B^T KI E_tau(s) G(s) J0(s)^(-1) KI B.
The determinant lemma gives the exact finite identity
  det Jt(s)/det J0(s)=det[I-diag(t)L(s)].
For a single edge it reduces to 1-t_e L_ee(s). The exterior/open hidden factors
are unchanged across these edge updates; retain their determinant factors when
counting full roots. If a Schur chart has poles inside a contour, count its
zero-minus-pole index and explicitly restore those fixed hidden contributions.
Contour points themselves must be regular. No truncated delay or walk series
is needed.

Finite cardinality exclusion theorem.

Choose a closed bounded contour Gamma whose entire interior lies in the forbidden
half-plane Re s> -sigma, with at least one full baseline NONROTATIONAL root and
none on its boundary. Exclude structural gauge roots from the protected region
or deflate them exactly before assigning its forbidden root count. Take verified
nonnegative bounds
  M_ij(s) >= bar_t_i * |L_ij(s)|
and one positive vector w over candidate edges. Let top_k denote the sum of the
k largest members of a nonnegative list. If
  sup_(s in Gamma) max_i top_k({M_ij(s)*w_j}_j)/w_i < 1,             (A)
then EVERY allowed intervention using at most k edges retains the same number
of full roots inside Gamma; in particular it cannot achieve the required margin.

Proof: for a support S with |S|<=k, the weighted infinity norm of
diag(t_S)L_SS(s) is bounded by the corresponding row sums, each bounded by (A).
This remains <1 throughout the homotopy t -> u*t, 0<=u<=1. Thus the determinant
ratio never vanishes on Gamma, its boundary homotopy has zero winding change,
and the full characteristic root count is unchanged. Fixed eliminated poles
cancel between endpoints once included correctly. The positive forbidden count
therefore persists. The argument is independent of which subset S was chosen.
Alternative subset-specific bounds can sharpen (A); failure of (A) proves nothing.

A certified stable (k+1)-edge design establishes that k+1 is the minimum number
within this FIXED-BASE, bounded edge-update family. A full-region DDE certificate,
with high-frequency exclusion and gauge treatment, is needed for the upper
witness; merely moving the one targeted pole is insufficient. Nonlinear event
validation remains a separate operational requirement.

Exact edge selection derivative and a structural no-authority case.

For a simple baseline root lambda with right/left vectors v,u of J0 and
d=u*J0_s(lambda)v nonzero, the exact first derivative is
  d lambda/d t_e = lambda*(u* KI b_e)
                  *(b_e^T KI E_tau(lambda)G(lambda)v)/d.           (B)
This is ordinary NEP sensitivity applied to the physical edge stamp. The two
factors expose actuation receptivity and the edge difference of a transformed
PLL error; physical graph distance or Fiedler value alone cannot give its sign.
It can rank candidates, but a large derivative is not a finite repair certificate.

There is also an exact finite obstruction: if
  B^T KI E_tau(lambda)G(lambda)v=0,
then Jt(lambda)v=0 for EVERY edge-weight vector t. Equivalently, the transformed
critical PLL-error vector is constant on each allowed communication component.
No controller in this edge family can remove that pole, regardless of gain.
Similarly u* KI B=0 preserves a left nullvector for every t. For one edge either
zero factor in (B) preserves the pole for all its finite gains. For several
edges, separate per-edge zero sensitivities do NOT by themselves imply this
global obstruction: higher-order combinations can act unless a shared left or
right annihilation condition holds. Exact equalities need structural evidence;
near-coherence gives small local authority, not an exact impossibility theorem.

Focused falsification test, not a broad simulation campaign.

Freeze one documented latency-stressed IEEE39 design, one protected forbidden
root contour, the original local gains, and a small declared engineering edge
set with finite gain limits. Compute (B) and exact single-edge determinants;
evaluate (A) for k=1 (and only then k=2 if meaningful). Search for an upper
witness in the same frozen family, check exact moments and full DDE spectrum,
and compare with the best bounded diagonal retuning at identical requirements.
If the lower bound is vacuous, if no stable upper witness exists, or if diagonal
retuning provides the same desired output more cheaply, retain that negative
result. Do not enlarge bounds or alter the moment objective after observing it.

Most important scope restriction: this does NOT prove that neighbors/communication
are necessary when all 20 local gains can be retuned. Such a claim requires a
uniform exclusion over that entire admissible local-gain family, including its
nonlinear spectral dependence; a failed local optimizer or fixed-gain lower
bound is insufficient. If local gains vary in a certified region, (A) may be
extended only with uniform L/count/hidden-chart bounds over the whole region.
Likewise an SG-retention alternative changes the plant and moment weights and
requires a separate chart; it cannot inherit the fixed-rho proof.

5. WHAT PDF4 CONTRIBUTES, AND WHAT NOT TO IMPORT

PDF4 is a robotics audit, not a power-system theorem. Its relevant lesson is
precise: physical authority/feasibility, recursive safety, progress and optimizer
success are different claims (pp3,10-14). Its Farkas examples certify the stated
QP, not every possible physical controller; the same scope rule applies to a
PLL-gain exclusion. Its delayed-consensus lambda_max law (p13) belongs to a
uniform-delay scalar consensus model and must not be transplanted to this DDAE.
One could study safe replacement paths rather than any-order blocker avoidance,
but that is a separate future problem. It is not needed to justify the single
focused theorem/design target above.

FINAL RECOMMENDATION

Use PDF3's model-scoped H4/contextual-return evidence as an existing anchor and
PDF1's exact moment law as the architectural constraint. Correct PDF5's explicit
mathematical overclaims before using it as theory. The strongest achievable next
target is a nonvacuous finite lower/upper architecture decision under declared
physical bounds. Until that target succeeds, neither expanded calculus vocabulary
nor a new collection of graph diagnostics supplies a stronger engineering result.
