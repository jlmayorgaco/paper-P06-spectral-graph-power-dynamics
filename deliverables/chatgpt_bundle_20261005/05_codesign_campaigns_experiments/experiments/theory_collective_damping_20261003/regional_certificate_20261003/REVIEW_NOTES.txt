MATHEMATICAL SELF-REVIEW AND CLAIM BOUNDARY

1. The root-cluster formula uses full holomorphic characteristic operators.
   A Schur complement with poles would require pole accounting. The descriptor
   lift is holomorphic and det(G) is independent of s; its roots coincide with
   the original characteristic roots only where G is nonsingular.
2. Norm <1 is required on every contour point and every design in the region.
   It selects a continuous trace-log branch and preserves algebraic root count.
   Internal repeated roots are permitted; roots crossing the contour are not.
3. The negative sign in the root-sum formula follows integration by parts and
   was checked against Delta=s-a-p. Averaging never implies each root is stable.
4. The affine lift is exact only for the inspected fixed-support sharing chart.
   General operating-point changes need additional terms. Gains are ordinary,
   not logarithmic. Delays are fixed coefficients throughout each certificate.
5. The descriptor remainder H_rem=0 does not mean eigenvalue remainder=0.
   The nonzero log-series bound and numerical-integration errors remain.
6. Positive-vector bounds are valid because |Theta*T|<=diag(radii)*|T|.
   Repeated rho correlation is conservatively relaxed for this bound.
   The original ten replacement variables remain tied in the regional LP.
7. The finite LP is a NECESSARY relaxation. Missing event constraints and
   outside roots can make it optimistic, not falsely restrictive. A rigorous
   upper bound still needs rigorous input coefficients, remainder and LP error.
8. A positive dual weight annihilating first-order gain sensitivities does not
   establish finite impossibility: the remainder must be included before a
   strict separating inequality can be claimed.
9. Both scalar and positive-vector envelopes are evaluated only at three
   fixed frequencies in TABLE_02/TABLE_03. No entire contour, cluster integral,
   stable heterogeneous-delay design or MW certificate is claimed.
10. The trace expansion is over dense dynamical intervention channels.
    Closed walks are not automatically physical line cycles; the operator is
    not a damping matrix or Laplacian. Its diagonal blocks already contain the
    full grid. Graph-basis truncation requires a separately bounded error.
