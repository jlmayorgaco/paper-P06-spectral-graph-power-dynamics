THEORY CLOSURE — 3 October 2026

PRIMARY DECISION
Develop the all-PLL return relation and an analytical gain map before another
design campaign. It is a full-linearization eigenstructure construction with
pure delays, not an exact nonlinear safety certificate.

PROBLEM: increasing SG replacement changes the entire network/device return.
GAP TO CLOSE HERE: the earlier one-site retuning law did not supply simultaneous
gains for all sites at a new replacement and prescribed collective pattern.
INSIGHT: at fixed pole and pattern, all local PI gains follow from complex
division; several patterns impose an exact real compatibility condition.
FALSIFYING TEST: the construction cannot reproduce known gains, or its restricted
pattern conflicts do not explain useful designs after patterns are allowed to vary.
CONSEQUENCE IF VALIDATED: predict admissible retuning and choose informative
neighbor signals using the full return, with communication delay included.

WHAT IS CLOSED MATHEMATICALLY
1. Simultaneous opening of all PLL triples; exact F(s) with no Pade.
2. Explicit all-site gain map for a prescribed nonreal pole and PLL pattern.
3. Necessary/sufficient local gain compatibility for several fixed patterns.
4. Necessary/sufficient added-signal range test and minimum weighted extra gain.
5. Exact frequency-dependent commutator obstruction for invertible polynomial
   graph filters applied to delayed source errors.
6. Gain-map derivatives and a declared continuation/SQP predictor-corrector.

ADVERSARIAL SCOPE REVIEW
- G must be formed after opening all PLLs. The previous single-site G_i depends
  on the other controllers and cannot simply be substituted into the all-site law.
- Nonsingular eliminated blocks and nonzero detector responses are essential.
- Gains, equilibrium and physical state support must follow the actual model.
- The eigenvector is a physical PLL-angle pattern, not automatically a Laplacian
  eigenvector. Lossy heterogeneous networks need not diagonalize in a graph basis.
- Assignment can succeed while unassigned poles are unstable. Pole multiplicity
  and conditioning also need checks.
- An infeasible fixed-pattern target is not a bound on all local stabilization.
- The neighbor formula assumes unbounded self gains. With any actual bounds,
  the exact constrained linear feasibility/QP problem is required.
- Neighbor feedback changes the controller architecture, exposes new signals,
  and adds physical communication latency. Its cost/bounds must be fixed first.
- Full-rank modal samples are required; a redundant neighbor adds no capability.
- A norm on raw coefficients is a declared tuning metric, not automatically
  actual actuator effort. Fix physical signal scaling and gain normalization.
- No structural fixed-mode or global replacement theorem follows from these
  compatibility tests. Simple counting is not a controllability proof.
- The nonlinear operational metric definitions are retained. No claim about
  nadir, windowed RoCoF, voltage or actuator margin follows from assignment.
- Allowing the full pattern to vary does not reduce dimension automatically.
- All linear-algebra/projection steps have established precedents; novelty
  depends on the PLL/network-specific result and its demonstrated usefulness.

LITERATURE CAPABILITY BOUNDARY (primary sources checked)

Huang, Xin, Dong, Doerfler, 2019; arXiv:1903.05489.
Full text previously read. Network Kron reduction, modal PLL/grid analysis,
retuning and IEEE-39 already exist. Their modal separation uses explicit
homogeneity assumptions; those assumptions cannot be imposed on this model.

Ram, Mottershead, Tehrani, 2011; DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2010.07.014.
Publisher PDF accessed via University of Groningen:
https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/173361406/1_s2.0_S0024379510003794_main.pdf
Sections 1-3 inspected. Symmetric second-order mechanical plant, state feedback
and receptances; partial delayed pole assignment with preservation of original
unassigned modes. This is a direct ancestor of the assignment/spillover idea.
It is not our nonlinear SG/GFL model or a nonlinear security frontier.

Katewa and Pasqualetti, 2021; DOI 10.1109/TAC.2020.3018615.
Author-hosted publisher PDF, sections I-III inspected:
https://cps.iisc.ac.in/faculty/vaibhav/Papers/2021TACMininumGainPolePlacement.pdf
Sparse static state feedback, minimum-gain pole/eigenstructure assignment,
analytical local conditions and iterative projected methods. Joint eigenvectors
and feedback make the problem nonconvex. Discusses fixed modes and restrictions
of sparse static controllers. We cannot claim eigenstructure projection or
sparse feedback optimization as a new mathematical principle.

Vanbiervliet, Verheyden, Michiels, Vandewalle, 2008;
DOI 10.1051/cocv:2007060.
Publisher/archive PDF abstract and model inspected:
https://www.numdam.org/item/10.1051/cocv:2007060.pdf
Fixed delays, parameter-dependent retarded systems, nonsmooth spectral-abscissa
optimization, and continuation of nonlinear equilibria. This prevents claiming
that fixed-delay spectral continuation itself is new.

Mao and Dai, 2013; DOI 10.3934/naco.2013.3.207.
Publisher record/abstract inspected, not full algorithm:
https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/naco.2013.3.207
Partial eigenvalue assignment with delay-robustness sensitivity and analytic
gradient optimization. No detailed head-to-head algorithm claim is made.

Coffey, Harrison, Egea-Alvarez, Xu, 2025;
DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3586197.
Author-repository full PDF, abstract/model-comparison scope inspected:
https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/93452/1/Coffey-etal-IEEEAccess-2025-Assessing-the-capability-and-limitations-of-small-signal-simplified.pdf
Compares full, reduced and simplified GFL models; reduced descriptions miss
important subsynchronous dynamics in some parameter regions. Supports the
full-model validation requirement; does not validate our own reduction.

Chakraborty, Pal, Maleki, 2025; arXiv:2505.24204.
Primary preprint PDF abstract/introduction inspected:
https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.24204
GFL plant power-oscillation damping versus GFM and PSS in a two-area system.
An auxiliary P/Q action differs from the permitted PLL-only action set.
It is an architecture comparator, not a reproduced baseline.
