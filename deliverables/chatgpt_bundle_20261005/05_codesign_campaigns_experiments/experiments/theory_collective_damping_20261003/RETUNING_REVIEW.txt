Scope audit of the theory-only extension

The scalar return relation opens the selected PLL, not the full converter.
It retains the other delays and the actual lossy P/Q and device dynamics.
The regularity and visibility assumptions cannot be dropped.

Exact pole assignment is not stabilization. The target pole can be stable
while another mode is unstable. The interval for positive gains is not a
maximum stable delay. Exogenous delays are never optimization variables.

The spillover identity concerns full complex pole preservation by one site's
two real gains. It does not rule out preserving two real parts, or compensation
by gains at several sites. Nonzero complex movement need not worsen damping.

The first-order replacement formula is exact for its own finite smooth
constraint model, a fixed replacement direction and a bounded gain set.
It is not a global SG replacement limit. Uniform remainder and all-constraint
coverage would be required for a regional full-model upper bound.

The contour condition is a conditional sufficient statement. No supremum
bound, root count or full nonlinear event test is computed in this extension.
The initial curve cannot have a zero on the contour; gauge removal is real
work, not notation. Full rho changes require the complete model difference.

The current claim ledger labels all these scopes. The research novelty
comparison remains open; ordinary pole placement and convex separation
are not being claimed as new mathematics.

Verification: thirteen exact symbolic checks passed. No ODE/DDE trajectory,
parameter sweep, root continuation, grid test or optimizer was run.
