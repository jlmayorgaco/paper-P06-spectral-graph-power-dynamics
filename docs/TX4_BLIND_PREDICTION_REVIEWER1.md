# Reviewer 1 — novelty and evidence audit

## Question

Is there actual novelty beyond robust-control stability radius, eigenvalue
sensitivity, impedance return ratios, and ordinary planning?

## Assessment

The mathematical ingredients are classical. The defensible contribution is the
combination of a finite discrete SG-to-GFL replacement question, a pre-reveal
reduced prediction from one network kernel plus local substitutions, an exact
contextual-return interpretation, and a full-order/TDS check. V4 is unusually
clean: 16/16 verdicts, exact H4 and kappa=4. The V9 result is a necessary
negative: 77.15% accuracy, 116 false-safe cases, and no exact H recovery.

The g-boundary agreement is numerically excellent, but its reduced search was
executed after reveal. It should be called a reduced reproduction, not a strict
blind boundary prediction. The reduced runtime is also worse than full order.

Scores (0--10): novelty 7; correctness 8; model adequacy 6; evidence 8;
usefulness 7; IAS impact 8; TPWRS readiness 5.

## Recommendation

Accept as a strong, carefully scoped IAS poster under CASE B. Require the
poster to foreground “model-specific” and “flagship” and to show V9 as a
negative transfer result. Do not use “first”, “unique”, “universal”, or
“general predictor”. A journal submission would need broader models,
pre-reveal boundary execution, and an independent custom-GFL implementation.
