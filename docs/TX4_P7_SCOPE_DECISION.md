# TX4 P7 scope decision

## Decision

`SUPPLEMENT ONLY`.

P7 is not a valid poster-level validation of the approximately 0.62 Hz
collective mechanism. It remains useful as a supplemental composition
counterexample, but its original interpretation must be removed from the
main poster.

## Evidence under the frozen P4 policy

| portfolio | global alpha [s^-1] | global frequency [Hz] | global class | alpha_EM [s^-1] | EM frequency [Hz] | status |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 30+31+32+34+36+38 | +422.835508 | 0.000000 | APERIODIC_REAL | -0.531301 | 1.377363 | global unstable, EM-band stable |
| 31+32+34+35+37+38 | -0.151122 | 1.320285 | TARGET_EM_BAND | -0.151122 | 1.320285 | stable |

The pair has the intended near-matched aggregate composition, but the
unstable member is dominated by a huge real aperiodic mode rather than by the
validated approximately 0.62 Hz collective mode. The pair therefore cannot
be used to claim an EM-band composition effect or a general penetration-only
failure. It may be shown in a supplement with the full global/EM split and
with the word `SUPPLEMENT ONLY`.

## Poster action

Remove the old P7 aggregate-pair panel from the main poster. Preserve the
underlying CSV and this decision document for auditability.
