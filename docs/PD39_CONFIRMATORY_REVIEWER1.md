# PD39 confirmatory campaign — Reviewer 1

## Brief assessment

The campaign verifies an exact finite discovery result: all 255 proper
portfolios pass the frozen nine-condition 0.05 s^-1 screen, while V8 fails it.
However, the result is not yet evidence that a new stability-radius theory has
been established. The structured-radius experiment was protocol-limited to 16
directions, did not finish `missing_38`, and did not include the matched-alpha
representative pairs required for the hidden-fragility hypothesis.

## Reviewer attack: novelty beyond existing methods

The potentially novel contribution is the co-design framing that separates
nominal eigenvalue margin, scenario margin, and a physically normalized
structured boundary search in an SG-to-GFL transition. In the executed data,
the strongest new observation is that nominal V8 is almost marginal
(`m_alpha=0.0014448`) and has a small boundary found at approximately
`rho_0=0.001906`, whereas several 7/8 cases have much larger found boundaries.
This is suggestive, not confirmatory of a hidden-fragility law.

The following remain classical or incomplete: eigenvalue stability screening,
participation factors, finite scenario enumeration, controller/line grid
searches, and ordinary penetration planning. No dynamic weak-node/link rank
campaign was completed, so the paper cannot claim a demonstrated superiority
over static weak-grid metrics on that axis.

## Recommendation

Narrow the claim to an auditable PD39 case study and numerical warning about
the distinction between true instability and a 0.05 s^-1 engineering margin.
Do not market H3, H4, H6, or H7 as passed. A full paper should add the missing
radius matched-pair, node, link, joint-repair, and validated-design analyses.
